Privacy Policy

3 Ladies Nails & Spa · 805 Glynn St S #135, Fayetteville, GA 30214 · Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you contact or book with 3 Ladies Nails & Spa, and how we use it. This page is a general summary for customers, not legal advice.

Information we collect

When you book or contact us by phone, by text, in person, or through the booking form on this website, we may collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, preferred service, preferred technician, and appointment time. This website does not require you to create an account, and it does not ask you for payment information online.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to arrange, confirm, and respond to your appointment and service questions, and to contact you about your booking. We do not sell your personal information.

Messages through Google Search and Maps

If you contact us through messaging on Google Search or Maps, your message may be handled by Google's AI messaging service to answer common questions and help with bookings, and more complex questions may be passed to our team. Google's own services are also covered by Google's privacy policy. Messages sent by text are subject to your mobile carrier's standard message rates.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, or would like us to correct the contact details we hold for your booking, please call (678) 489-6750 or text (404) 509-2709.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current version.
